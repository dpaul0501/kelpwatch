description: Explain the current El Niño or La Niña state and what it means for the Pacific Northwest and kelp.
---
1. In one step, call get_enso_status and get_sea_temperature together.
2. Report the latest Oceanic Niño Index value, its season, the phase and strength, using the thresholds the tool gives.
3. Mention the satellite cross-check and whether the two sources agree; explain that OISST runs warmer because of its older baseline.
4. Connect to kelp carefully: give the recent Salish Sea summer temperature anomalies from the data, and say that marine heat is a documented kelp stressor. Do not predict kelp outcomes.
5. Say that the ONI lags current conditions by about a month.
