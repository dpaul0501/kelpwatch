---
name: drought-advisory
description: Assess current drought and crop stress for farmers in India.
---
1. Call get_drought_conditions.
2. Report 30-day rainfall, the 1991-2020 normal for the same days, and the percent of normal, with the date the data ends.
3. Report mean vegetation health (NDVI) and its date.
4. Use the tool's risk category and explain the thresholds it used.
5. Note that CHIRPS lags real time by several weeks, so conditions today may differ.
